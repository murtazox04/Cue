    Importance and expiry¶

    Phones now decide for themselves what deserves attention. iOS has interruption levels and AI summaries, and Android's Notification Organizer silences promotions by default. A message that says how important it is, and when it stops mattering, is handled better by the device. It also gives Cue's policy something real to decide on.

    Importance¶

    Level Use it for iOS Android Telegram E-mail
    low Informational: a weekly summary, a new follower passive (no sound) normal priority silent Importance: low
    normal Most messages (default) active channel default normal none
    high Time-sensitive: "your order is at the door" time-sensitive high priority normal Importance: high
    critical Security and safety: a login from a new device time-sensitive high priority normal Importance: high

    Policy treats critical differently from the rest: it breaks through quiet hours and frequency caps. To stop that being abused, it is only honoured in categories people cannot unsubscribe from (allow_unsubscribe: false). Anywhere else it is stored and delivered as high. Unsubscribes, muted channels, cooldowns and deduplication always apply.

    Set importance on a rule, or on a direct send:

    POST /v1/rules
{"key": "new-login", "event": "auth.login_new_device", "template": "new-login",
 "category": "security", "channels": ["push", "email"], "importance": "critical"}

    POST /v1/messages
{"recipient": "user-42", "template": "courier-arriving", "channels": ["push"],
 "importance": "high", "expires_at": "2026-10-10T18:30:00Z"}

    Templates can read it as {{ importance }}, and connectors receive it as delivery.importance. The webhook payload includes it too.

    Expiry¶

    A one-time code is useless after five minutes, and "your driver is here" is wrong an hour later. Give such messages an expiry:

    • direct sends: expires_at;
    • rules: expires_after_seconds, counted from when the event is processed.

    Cue drops the message (suppressed, reason expired) if quiet hours would delay it past its expiry, and checks again right before sending, so a slow worker or a provider outage never delivers stale news. Push connectors pass the expiry on to the push service as well (FCM ttl, APNs apns-expiration), so a phone that was offline does not show it later.

    Actions¶

    Templates can add up to four buttons. Their label and url are templates:

    "en": {
  "title": "Your order shipped",
  "body": "Order {{ data.order_id }} is on its way.",
  "actions": [
    {"id": "track", "label": "Track", "url": "https://shop.example/track/{{ data.order_id }}"},
    {"id": "dismiss", "label": "Not now"}
  ]
}
    Connector What it does with actions
    fcm data.actions (JSON) for your app to render as notification actions
    telegram Inline keyboard buttons for actions with a url
    smtp "Label: URL" lines in the text part
    webhook content.actions, as is
