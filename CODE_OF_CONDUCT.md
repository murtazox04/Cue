# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion
focused on the work. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub](https://github.com/murtazox04/Cue/security/advisories/new) (choose
"Report a vulnerability" and describe the conduct issue) or by direct message to a
maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.
